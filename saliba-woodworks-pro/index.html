Our workshop

A small shop in the heart of Floriana

Saliba Woodworks is a family carpentry workshop that has quietly served Floriana and the surrounding towns for years. We keep the shop small on purpose — it means the person who measures your job is the same one who cuts, joins and finishes it.

We work mostly in solid timber, with proper joinery and honest prices. If a repair is the right answer, we'll tell you — and if something can't be done well, we won't pretend otherwise.

— Chris Saliba, master carpenter
